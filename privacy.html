Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what the Dynamic Facts website and publishing tool do with information.

This website

This website does not use cookies, does not ask for personal information, and has no accounts or login.

What the publishing tool accesses

The tool is used only by the owner of Dynamic Facts. With the owner's permission it accesses the owner's own TikTok and YouTube account authorisation tokens, and basic profile information (such as account name) so it can upload the owner's own videos. It may also read public statistics (views, likes, comments) of the owner's own videos.

What we store

Authorisation tokens, kept in a private repository controlled by the owner. We do not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or any other third party.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time in their TikTok or Google account settings.
