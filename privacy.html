Privacy Policy

This application is used only by the owner of the Dynamic Facts to publish the owner's own videos.

What we access: the owner's TikTok and YouTube account authorisation tokens, and public statistics (views, likes, comments) of the owner's own videos.

What we store: authorisation tokens, in a private repository controlled by the owner. We do not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or any other third party.

Revoking access: the owner can remove the application's access at any time in their TikTok or Google account settings.
