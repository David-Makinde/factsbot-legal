About Dynamic Facts

Dynamic Facts is a facts channel run by a single creator. Our goal is simple: make learning about the world quick, accurate and fun.

How our videos are made

Each video starts from a reference article. We write a short script using only what that source states, then check every claim against it before publishing. Videos combine a synthetic voiceover with licensed stock footage and on-screen captions. We label AI-assisted content where platforms ask us to.

How we publish

We use a private tool, built for our own accounts only, to send finished videos to our own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it only works for accounts that the owner has personally authorised. It does not collect data from viewers.

Corrections

Spotted a mistake? Tell us at dynamicfunfacts@gmail.com and we will fix or remove the video.
